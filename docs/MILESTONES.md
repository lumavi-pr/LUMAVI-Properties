# How we got here

LUMAVI is a young company with a long runway behind it: a founder with 15 years of project management and solutions architecture, years of field inspections for mortgage servicers, and a family house that had been closed for three decades. This is the build log since then.

## 2025

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fall 2025** | **LUMAVI opens.** First rescue: the founder's own inherited family house in Bayamón, closed for more than 30 years. |

## 2026

| Date | Milestone |
|---|---|
| **June** | Work starts on what becomes **Lumova**, LUMAVI's own platform — one system for the website, the families and the team. |
| **By Aug** | The team works from its own workspace: leads, cases, properties and field reports in one place. |
| **Oct 3** | Expenses, inspection-to-quote and the investor view join the workspace. |
| **Oct 5** | **Client portal launches** — families sign in with a one-time code and follow their case. |
| **Oct 8** | New home page, chosen by the team by unanimous vote. Every product decision starts being written down with its reason. |
| **Oct 13** | Case page rebuilt around what the team actually needs on the phone. Public price page. |
| **Oct 16** | The team platform gets its name, **Lumova**, and becomes its own installable app; the client portal installs as the LUMAVI app. Consultation-to-client conversion is tracked weekly. |
| **Oct 18** | **Inheritance guide for heirs living in the States**, in English and Spanish. Investors can make offers online. |
| **Oct 20** | Rent tracking per house. A response-time clock measures how long every client waits on us. |
| **Oct 25** | One link for everything: **[lumavipr.com/app](https://lumavipr.com/app)**. |
| **Oct 26** | The blog gets its own English pages. |
| **Oct 27** | The 8-page paper family form becomes a guided phone form. Live field check-ins for clients. **Río Piedras goes under contract.** |
| **Oct 30** | The CRM begins: every person and every conversation in one place, from first call to closed case. |
| **Oct 31** | Money board, property phases, profit per property, a public list of homes for sale and a **tenant app**. |
| **Nov 1–2** | **Online payments** on consultations, property checks and applications, in the customer's language. One-tap invoicing from inside a case. One visual system across the whole site and portal. |
| **Nov 3** | **AI document reader** for incoming case papers. Client update summaries, approved by a person before they go out. Blog readable by search engines and AI assistants. |
| **Nov 4** | This showcase — and Lumova's first public appearance. |
